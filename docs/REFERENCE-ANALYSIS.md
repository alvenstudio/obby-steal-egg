**Exit test:** *Every biome is distinguishable from a single screenshot with the UI hidden.*

### M6 — Pet Roster + Index (3 weeks)
**Build:** All 110 biome pets + 18 event pets modelled to the §3.1 family rules. Rarity nameplates, size/weight display, mutation shaders (Silver, Golden, Rainbow, Bloom, Spirit Bloom, Mecha). Pet Index UI with silhouettes, per-pet CLAIM, biome completion → Bat skin. Fuse Machine with the §4.9 odds table.

**Exit test:** *A player can name three pets they want and say why.* If the roster is interchangeable, the visual descriptions in §3 weren't followed.

**Parallelization note:** pets are the largest art bill in the project (128 models). Start M6 art in parallel with M3 — the gameplay code needs only a placeholder capsule per pet until M6 integration. Sequence the art queue as: **Forest → Lake → Desert (needed for M3) → guardians (needed for M5) → everything else**.

### M7 — Single-Player Systems (2 weeks)
**Build:** Heat system with nest pips. Tiered failure (drop/crack/salvage/total). Rotating daily nest modifiers. Ghost runs + drop markers. Scheduled opt-in Pen raids. Daily Rival NPC.

**Exit test:** *A player who has farmed the same nest six times in a row is visibly having a harder and more interesting time than on run one — and knows why.*

### M8 — Progression & Meta (2 weeks)
**Build:** Full treadmill 1–6 with the Speed cap at tier 6. All 10 trail tiers. Pen slots 4→20 on the §4.5 curve. Rebirth 1–8. Sell Stand. Free Gift panel. Offline income banking with the return panel. Great Bloom / Sakura Incubator. Hungry Monster event + Monster Chest table.

**Exit test:** *A fresh save reaches Rebirth 1 in ≤ 50 minutes and Rebirth 3 in ≤ 5 hours, without AFK.* If it takes longer, the Trial rewards in §9.6 are too low.

### M9 — Polish & Ship (2 weeks)
**Build:** Full audio pass (§6.8, all five layers, per-guardian barks). Every VFX in §7.7. Complete accessibility settings (§6.14). Third-person Pen mode + Photo Mode. Visitable pen share links. Nest leaderboards. Mobile touch controls + performance tier detection.

**Exit test:** *Ships at 60 fps on a 2020 mid-range laptop at 1080p and 30 fps on a 3-year-old phone, with the directional threat arc readable at both.*

### 10.3 The five risks, named

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **First-person platforming is illegible** — players can't see their feet, hate the game, bounce in 30 s | **CRITICAL** | M0 exists solely to de-risk this. Visible legs + landing marker + edge peek + −80° pitch clamp are all mandatory, not optional. Do not proceed past M0 on a maybe. |
| **The carried egg blocks the screen** and ruins platforming | HIGH | The three-part mitigation in §6.5 (tuck pose + transparency ramp + shoulder carry) must ship together. Test carrying a Giant egg through the Cosmic Trial specifically. |
| **128 pet models is the whole art budget** and it slips | HIGH | Start the art queue at week 3, in parallel. Ship with 60 pets if needed (drop the two newest biomes + event pools) — the §3 tables mark exactly which. |
| **The skill-gate economy is mistuned** and players stall or fly | MEDIUM | The Trial reward table (§9.6) is the single tuning knob. Instrument time-to-each-biome from day one and expect to retune it three times. |
| **Motion sickness** in a young audience | MEDIUM | §6.14 accessibility settings ship in M0, not M9. Default head-bob to **Reduced**, not Full. |

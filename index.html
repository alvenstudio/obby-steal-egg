EGG HEIST

Eleven biomes. One hundred and seven pets. Something enormous asleep beside every nest.

WASD move
Shift sprint
Space jump
Ctrl crouch
E steal / use
Esc close panel

Speed is the only key in this game. It opens every biome, and it is the only thing that gets you back out. Train it on the treadmill in your garden, then go take something that is not yours.
